Privacy policy

Short version: DigiClean doesn't collect anything. It has no account, no analytics and no servers of its own.

The app

  • Scanning, fingerprinting and comparing files happen entirely on your device.
  • The app makes no network connections. It sends no file names, fingerprints, settings, crash reports or usage data anywhere, and does not check for updates online.
  • What it stores (settings, scan caches, the Recovery Bin) stays on your device, in your user profile. Uninstalling removes the program; your settings and Recovery Bin are kept until you delete them.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.
  • To show the latest version number, your browser asks GitHub's public API (api.github.com) for the newest release. Downloads are served by GitHub Releases. Those requests go to GitHub and are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The server hosting this page may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and troubleshooting.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub.

Terms of use

  • Free to use. DigiClean is free to download and use, at home or at work. The source code is published on GitHub under the licence stated there.
  • You decide what is removed. DigiClean always keeps at least one copy in every group and, by default, moves removed items to the Recycle Bin / Trash or its Recovery Bin. You are still responsible for your files: review what you remove and keep backups of anything important.
  • No warranty. DigiClean is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, the authors are not liable for any loss of data or other damage arising from its use.
  • Downloads. Get DigiClean from this site or its GitHub releases. You can check every file against the published SHA-256 checksums.
